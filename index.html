The Story

Santiago, a young shepherd in Andalusia, keeps dreaming of a treasure hidden at the pyramids of Egypt. Urged on by a mysterious king, he sells his flock and sets out. Robbed in Tangier, he rebuilds his life in a crystal shop, crosses the desert with a caravan, finds love at an oasis, and learns from an alchemist to listen to his heart and the world around him. At the pyramids he discovers that the treasure was buried back where his journey began, but he could only learn that by making the journey.

What it teaches

  • Follow your own path, even when it's uncertain.
  • Setbacks are part of the journey, not the end of it.
  • Love should free you, not hold you back.
  • The journey itself changes you.
